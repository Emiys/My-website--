Protecting My Peace: I’m Growing, Glowing, and Logging Out of Nonsense

November 22, 2025

This season feels different. Maybe it’s growth, maybe it’s God, maybe it’s the fact that I’m tired of dealing with people who act like loading screens.

I’ve learned something huge: some people leave your life and suddenly everything becomes quieter, clearer, lighter. You don’t even hate them—you just finally understand your worth.

I’m in my peaceful girl era. My soft-but-still-smart era. My “drink water, mind my business, and refuse stress” era. If your energy is confusing, I’m already halfway out the door mentally.

Letting go wasn’t easy. It stung a little, especially when I remembered the good parts. But choosing myself feels better than chasing someone who doesn’t know what they want.

I’m not HR for anyone’s emotional drama. I don’t fix unstable vibes. If your energy keeps glitching, kindly contact your manufacturer.

These days, I’m investing in friendships that feel like peace, not exams. People who pour back into me. People who don’t make me question my value.

God is teaching me to trust the exits as much as the blessings. When He removes someone, He’s protecting something in me.

So here I am: healing, leveling up, glowing quietly, and choosing peace like it’s a full-time job. No guilt. No apology.
